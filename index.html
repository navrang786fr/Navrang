Our Kitchen

Cooked slow, served hot

Every curry starts with hand-ground masala, every biryani is sealed and cooked on dum, and every starter comes off the fire to order. From egg curries and creamy paneer to Andhra-style chicken and fresh seafood, the menu spans the full range of a home-style Andhra kitchen — mild to fiery, vegetarian to non-veg, all made fresh.

Whether it's a quick fried-rice lunch or a full biryani spread for the table, Navarang keeps the recipes true to where they come from.
